01 / 04  — The Cohort

375 patients.
One disease.
Whole transcriptome.

TCGA-STAD bulk RNA-seq: 375 primary tumor samples plus 30 matched adjacent normals, harmonized to GRCh38. Comprehensive clinical annotation includes AJCC stage, grade, overall survival, and molecular subtype (EBV, MSI, CIN, GS).

02 / 04  — Differential Expression

1,247 genes
rewritten by
the tumor.

DESeq2 Wald test (FDR < 0.05, |log₂FC| > 1.5) identified 782 upregulated and 465 downregulated genes. Cell-cycle drivers MKI67, TOP2A, and PCNA dominate the upregulated landscape.

03 / 04  — Survival Signatures

MKI67 predicts
outcome.
HR = 1.74.

Kaplan-Meier and multivariate Cox modeling confirm MKI67-high patients face significantly worse overall survival (HR = 1.74, 95% CI 1.42–2.13, p < 0.001). GSEA revealed 14 enriched Hallmark gene sets — E2F and MYC targets lead.

Overview

Why Gastric
Adenocarcinoma?

STAD ranks among the deadliest GI malignancies globally, yet most patients still lack actionable molecular targets.

🎯
Clinical Motivation

A 5-year survival rate below 35% for stage III–IV disease and limited targeted therapies beyond HER2 and PD-1/PD-L1 make deeper molecular profiling a clinical necessity.

🧬
Multi-Resolution Design

Bulk RNA-seq from TCGA-STAD delivers population-level DE and survival associations; upcoming scRNA-seq from CZ CELLxGENE will resolve cell-type-specific expression programs.

📊
Analytical Scope

DESeq2, Kaplan-Meier survival modeling, GSEA against Hallmark and KEGG gene sets, and cell-type deconvolution — fully reproducible and version-controlled.

Project Timeline

Milestones

A reproducible pipeline built in deliberate stages.

Nov 2024 Complete
Data Acquisition

Downloaded TCGA-STAD cohort (N=375) via GDC Data Portal using TCGAbiolinks. HTSeq-Counts, GRCh38/hg38. Included 30 matched adjacent normals.

Dec 2024 Complete
QC & Preprocessing

Library QC with MultiQC and RSeQC. 18,342 of 60,483 features retained. DESeq2 median-of-ratios normalization. ComBat-seq batch correction evaluated.

Jan 2025 Complete
Differential Expression

DESeq2 Wald test: 1,247 DEGs at FDR < 0.05, |log₂FC| > 1.5. Top upregulated: MKI67, TOP2A, PCNA. Top downregulated: CLDN18, CDH1.

Feb 2025 Complete
Survival Analysis

Kaplan-Meier and log-rank testing for 50+ candidate genes. Multivariate Cox PH adjusted for stage, age, subtype. MKI67: HR 1.74 (p < 0.001).

Mar 2025 Complete
Pathway Enrichment (GSEA)

fgsea against MSigDB Hallmark and KEGG. 14 enriched sets (FDR < 0.25). Top: E2F Targets (NES +2.41), MYC Targets (NES +2.28), G2M Checkpoint (NES +2.19).

In Progress Active
scRNA-seq Integration

CZ CELLxGENE gastric cancer data acquisition underway. Seurat v5 QC, clustering, SingleR annotation. CIBERSORT + MuSiC deconvolution to map TME fractions.
