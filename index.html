Overview

Why Gastric
Adenocarcinoma?

STAD is the fifth most common malignancy worldwide, yet actionable molecular targets remain elusive for most patients.

🎯
Clinical Motivation

A 5-year survival rate below 35% for stage III–IV disease and limited actionable targets underscore the need for deeper transcriptomic characterization.

🧬
Scientific Approach

Multi-resolution framework: bulk RNA-seq from TCGA-STAD provides population-level DE and survival associations, while scRNA-seq will resolve cell-type-specific expression programs.

📊
Analytical Scope

DESeq2, Kaplan-Meier survival modeling, GSEA against Hallmark and KEGG gene sets, and cell-type deconvolution — all version-controlled in linked repositories.

Key Findings

01 / 03  — Differential Expression

1,247 genes
dysregulated in tumor

DESeq2 Wald test identified 782 upregulated and 465 downregulated genes (|log₂FC| > 1.5, FDR < 0.05) between primary tumor and adjacent normal tissue. Top hits include MKI67, TOP2A, and CLDN18.

02 / 03  — Survival Analysis

MKI67 predicts
poor prognosis

Elevated MKI67 expression is independently associated with poor overall survival in multivariate Cox modeling (HR = 1.74, 95% CI 1.42–2.13, p < 0.001), retaining significance after adjustment for AJCC stage, age, and molecular subtype.

03 / 03  — Pathway Enrichment

E2F & MYC programs
dominate STAD

GSEA identified 14 significantly enriched Hallmark gene sets (FDR < 0.25). E2F targets (NES +2.41), MYC targets (NES +2.28), and G2M checkpoint (NES +2.19) are the top upregulated transcriptional programs.
