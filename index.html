01 / 04  — The Cohort

375 patients.
One disease.
Whole transcriptome.

TCGA-STAD bulk RNA-seq: 375 primary tumor samples plus 30 matched adjacent normals, harmonized to GRCh38, with comprehensive clinical annotation including stage, grade, overall survival, and molecular subtype (EBV, MSI, CIN, GS).

02 / 04  — Differential Expression

1,247 genes
rewritten by
the tumor.

DESeq2 Wald test (FDR < 0.05, |log₂FC| > 1.5) identified 782 upregulated and 465 downregulated genes. Cell-cycle drivers MKI67, TOP2A, and PCNA dominate the upregulated landscape; tight-junction gene CLDN18 is among the most consistently silenced.

03 / 04  — Survival Signatures

MKI67 predicts
outcome with
HR = 1.74.

Kaplan-Meier stratification and multivariate Cox modeling confirm MKI67-high patients face significantly worse overall survival (HR = 1.74, 95% CI 1.42–2.13, p < 0.001), independent of AJCC stage, age, and molecular subtype. GSEA revealed 14 enriched Hallmark gene sets — E2F and MYC targets top the list.

CZ CELLxGENE scRNA-seq data acquisition is underway. Seurat v5 clustering, SingleR annotation against the Human Cell Atlas, and CIBERSORT deconvolution will resolve the tumor microenvironment at single-cell resolution — validating and extending bulk findings.

Overview

Why Gastric
Adenocarcinoma?

STAD ranks among the deadliest GI malignancies globally, yet most patients still lack actionable molecular targets.

🎯
Clinical Motivation

A 5-year survival rate below 35% for stage III–IV disease and limited targeted therapies beyond HER2 and PD-1/PD-L1 make deeper molecular profiling a clinical necessity.

🧬
Multi-Resolution Design

Bulk RNA-seq from TCGA-STAD provides population-level DE and survival associations; upcoming scRNA-seq from CZ CELLxGENE will resolve cell-type-specific expression programs.

📊
Analytical Scope

DESeq2 differential expression, Kaplan-Meier survival modeling, GSEA against Hallmark and KEGG gene sets, and cell-type deconvolution — fully reproducible and version-controlled.

Project Timeline

Milestones

A reproducible pipeline built in stages.

Nov 2024 Complete
Data Acquisition

Downloaded TCGA-STAD cohort (N=375) via GDC Data Portal using TCGAbiolinks. HTSeq-Counts, GRCh38/hg38 reference. Included 30 matched adjacent normal samples for contrast analysis.

Dec 2024 Complete
QC & Preprocessing

Library QC with MultiQC and RSeQC. Low-expressed genes filtered; 18,342 of 60,483 features retained. DESeq2 median-of-ratios normalization. Batch effects evaluated via ComBat-seq.

Jan 2025 Complete
Differential Expression

DESeq2 Wald test: 1,247 DEGs at FDR < 0.05, |log₂FC| > 1.5. Top upregulated: MKI67, TOP2A, PCNA. Top downregulated: CLDN18, CDH1. Volcano and MA plots generated with EnhancedVolcano.

Feb 2025 Complete
Survival Analysis

Kaplan-Meier estimator and log-rank testing for 50+ candidate genes. Multivariate Cox PH modeling adjusted for stage, age, and TCGA molecular subtype. MKI67: HR 1.74 (p < 0.001).

Mar 2025 Complete
Pathway Enrichment (GSEA)

fgsea with Wald-statistic ranking against MSigDB Hallmark (H) and KEGG (C2). 14 enriched gene sets at FDR < 0.25. Top: E2F Targets (NES +2.41), MYC Targets (NES +2.28), G2M Checkpoint (NES +2.19).

In Progress Active
scRNA-seq Integration

CZ CELLxGENE gastric cancer dataset acquisition underway. Seurat v5 QC, clustering, and SingleR annotation planned. CIBERSORT and MuSiC deconvolution to resolve TME cell-type fractions across TCGA cohort.
